Specialities

Six clinics, one standard of explanation

Each speciality runs to its own protocol and follow-up cadence. What they share is the written one-page plan you take home from every visit.

Preventive cardiology

Risk is calculable long before symptoms arrive. A first visit establishes your ten-year cardiac risk from blood pressure, lipids, family history, glucose handling and — where it changes the answer — a coronary calcium score.

  • Full lipid profile with apoB and Lp(a) where indicated
  • ECG and echocardiography on site
  • Statin decisions explained with absolute, not relative, numbers
  • Six-monthly review once you are stable

Hypertension clinic

Clinic readings alone overtreat some people and miss others. We start with a fortnight of home readings on a validated cuff, then titrate towards the lowest dose that holds your target — often fewer tablets than you arrived with.

  • Home-monitoring protocol and cuff-fitting check
  • 24-hour ambulatory monitoring when readings disagree
  • Secondary-cause screening for early or resistant cases
  • Salt, sleep and alcohol reviewed before adding a second drug

Diabetes & metabolic care

HbA1c is an average, and averages hide a great deal. Where it helps, a two-week continuous glucose sensor shows exactly which meals and which nights are driving your numbers — and makes the diet conversation specific instead of generic.

  • CGM-guided review with a dietitian in the same week
  • Annual retina, foot and kidney screening, tracked and reminded
  • Pre-diabetes reversal programme over twelve weeks
  • Cardiac and kidney protection weighed into every drug choice

Executive health check

One half-day, one physician, one conversation. Bloods and imaging in the morning, then a full reading of every line with you the same afternoon — including the values that are flagged but do not matter.

  • Panel chosen for your age, sex and history — not a fixed package
  • Same-day results discussion, never a separate follow-up fee
  • A one-page summary you can share with any other doctor
  • Corporate blocks available for small teams

Thyroid & hormonal

Tiredness, weight change and low mood are real symptoms with a long list of causes. They are worked up in order rather than attributed to the first borderline result — and a single abnormal TSH is repeated before anything is started.

  • Thyroid function, antibodies and ultrasound where warranted
  • Iron, B12, vitamin D and sleep assessed alongside
  • PCOS and insulin-resistance evaluation
  • Dose review six weeks after any change, never sooner

Second opinions

Bring whatever you have — reports, discharge summaries, a photograph of a prescription. You will leave with a plain-language read of what it says, whether the plan is reasonable, and what a different approach would mean.

  • Pre-procedure reviews before angioplasty or bypass
  • Medication rationalisation for long prescription lists
  • Written summary you can take back to your treating team
  • Available as a video consultation for out-of-city patients
What a visit looks like

Three steps, no surprises

01

History, unhurried

Twenty of the thirty minutes go to the conversation. Bring your reports; nothing gets repeated unnecessarily.

02

Examination and tests

Only what changes the plan, with the reason and the cost said out loud before anything is ordered.

03

A written plan

One page: findings, changes, what to watch for, and the date of your next review.
