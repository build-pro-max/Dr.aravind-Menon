Contact the clinic
Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book directly through the appointment form or by phone. If another doctor has referred you, bring their note so nothing is repeated unnecessarily.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Every report from the last two years, your current medicine strips or a photo of them, and any home blood-pressure or glucose readings you have been keeping.

How long is a consultation?

First visits are 30 minutes, reviews 15. The clinic caps its daily list precisely so those slots are not compressed.

Are video consultations available?

Yes, for reviews and report discussions. New complaints that need an examination are seen in person first.

Which insurers are accepted?

Cashless is available with most major Indian insurers for hospital procedures. OPD consultations are paid directly and a claimable receipt is issued.
