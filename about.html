The doctor

Eighteen years of listening first, prescribing second

Dr. Aravind Menon is a consultant physician with a superspecialisation in cardiology. He trained at St. John's Medical College in Bengaluru, completed his MD in internal medicine at AIIMS New Delhi, and his DM in cardiology at PGIMER Chandigarh.

He returned to Bengaluru in 2014 to open Meridian Health Clinic on a single rule: the daily list is capped. It is the only way a thirty-minute consultation stays thirty minutes.

Training & career

The path here

Two decades across three of India's largest teaching hospitals, then a deliberate move to small-practice medicine.

  1. 2001 – 2007

    MBBS · St. John's Medical College, Bengaluru

    Graduated with distinction in medicine; university gold medal in general pathology.

  2. 2008 – 2011

    MD Internal Medicine · AIIMS, New Delhi

    Thesis on early markers of metabolic syndrome in South Asian adults under 40.

  3. 2011 – 2014

    DM Cardiology · PGIMER, Chandigarh

    Trained in non-invasive cardiology, echocardiography and cardiac rehabilitation.

  4. 2014 – Present

    Founder & Consultant · Meridian Health Clinic

    A capped-list practice on Lavelle Road, with visiting rights at three Bengaluru hospitals.

  5. 2019 – Present

    Visiting faculty · Cardiac prevention module

    Teaches the post-graduate preventive cardiology rotation two mornings a month.

How the clinic works

Four commitments, kept in writing

01

A capped daily list

Sixteen patients a day, never more. That is what protects your slot from the one before it.

02

A written plan, every visit

One page: what we found, what changes, what to watch for, when to come back.

03

No test without a reason

Every investigation is justified out loud before it is ordered — including its cost.

04

Generic first, always

Prescriptions name the molecule, with the price difference written beside the brand.
